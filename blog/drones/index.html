Thirteen Years of Drones

My experience flying drones of all kinds

Digging through old computer backups is a great time. You get to launch apps you forgot even existed, find weird files from the trenches of rabbit holes, and of course, look at priceless pictures of times passed.

While looking through my old drone pictures, I realized almost none of my hobbies have seen a level of progress even comparable to what I’ve experienced with drones.

My first one (2013)

That’s 9 year old me! This was a few days after I got my Spycat Helicopter. It had of course been smashed into every side of my house and every other tree in the yard, but boy was that a fun summer.

I caught the tail end of the helicopter hobby. By this point, most helicopter pilots had moved on to quadcopters. I was of course oblivious to this and just enjoyed flying whatever I had.

Tiny drones! (2016)

By this point, I had collected a few 5 inch quadcopters but the hyped was entirely around making them small. The prospect of flying indoors was awesome, especially as someone that lived in a 4 season climate.

My drone was the CX-10. I flew at least 200 packs on this thing.

FPV Era (2018)

By this point the FPV hobby started going mainstream. The DRL (Drone Racing League) was picking up steam quite quickly.
